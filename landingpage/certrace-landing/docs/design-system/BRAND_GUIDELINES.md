# Certrace Brand Guidelines

A practical guide for non-designers. If you only remember one sentence:
**warm paper, near-black ink, light type, tiny green signals, lots of air.**

---

## 1. Brand Identity

### What makes Certrace visually recognizable

1. **The bone-white canvas** — `#F5F4F0`, never pure white, never gray-blue.
   Everything sits on this warm paper tone.
2. **Huge, thin headlines** — large IBM Plex Sans at *Light* weight, tightly
   tracked, sentence case with a period. Confidence through scale, not bold.
3. **Typewriter labels** — small UPPERCASE Courier Prime tags (`THE PROBLEM`,
   `CERTRACE`) with wide letter-spacing.
4. **Hairline everything** — cards and sections separated by barely-there
   borders, not shadows or color blocks.
5. **Tiny living signals** — small pulsing emerald/amber/red dots and ticking
   mono numbers that make the product feel alive.
6. **Generative monochrome art** — pixel icons, node networks, bar fields
   drawn in code. No photos, no 3D mascots, no clip art.

### Personality traits

Calm · precise · technical · trustworthy · understated · quietly optimistic.

### Brand perception goals

A compliance director should think: *"serious infrastructure, already
running, built by people who sweat details."* An investor should think:
*"category-defining, enterprise-grade."* Never: loud, startup-y, gimmicky.

## 2. Do's and Don'ts

### Do

- ✅ Use `#F5F4F0` as the background of *everything* — slides, social posts,
  one-pagers.
- ✅ Set headlines big and **Light** (300). Let whitespace do the talking.
- ✅ Use opacity steps of near-black for hierarchy (100% headline → 45% body →
  35% caption).
- ✅ Use UPPERCASE Courier Prime for small labels and the wordmark
  `C E R T R A C E` (wide letter-spacing).
- ✅ Keep color for *status only*: green = verified/live, amber = pending,
  red = overdue, blue = in progress — always as small dots, chips, or text.
- ✅ Round corners: 16px for cards/containers, 12px for buttons, pills for tags.
- ✅ Show real-looking data (supplier tables, calculation trails) instead of
  abstract illustrations.

### Don't

- ❌ No pure white or dark-mode backgrounds. No gradients as decoration.
- ❌ No bold/black headline weights, no italics, no ALL-CAPS headlines.
- ❌ No saturated color fills, colored buttons, or rainbow charts. The only
  dark fill allowed is the `#111` primary button.
- ❌ No stock photography, emoji in UI copy, drop shadows, glassmorphism cards
  (glass is reserved for the navbar), or bouncy animations.
- ❌ No more than one highlighted (`#F0EEE8`) card per group, one primary
  button per region.
- ❌ Never letter-space body text; never center long paragraphs.

## 3. Image Style

- **Illustration:** generative, monochrome ink-on-bone. Pixel-grid glyphs
  (8×8), node-and-line networks, dotted grids, bar fields. Accent allowed:
  emerald at ≤ 20% opacity.
- **Photography:** none on product surfaces. If unavoidable (press, careers),
  desaturate toward warm gray and place on the bone canvas with 16px-radius
  corners.
- **3D/render style (marketing only):** translucent dichroic glass objects on
  a pale `#F5F4F0` studio background with soft prismatic caustics — see
  [IMAGE-CONCEPTS.md](../../IMAGE-CONCEPTS.md) for ready prompts (glass cubes,
  prisms, column charts).
- **Icons:** 1.5px-stroke line icons in ink, inside 12px-radius bordered
  squares; or animated pixel glyphs for section identities.
- **Background treatment:** dotted grids (`rgba(17,17,17,0.045)` dots, 56px
  apart), 24px hairline grids, progressive blur masks that fade visuals into
  the canvas. Visuals never end with a hard edge — they dissolve.
- **Charts/data graphics:** ink bars and lines on hairline grids; emerald for
  the "good" series only; mono labels.

## 4. Marketing Asset Guidelines

**The universal recipe** — works for any format:

1. Fill the background `#F5F4F0`.
2. Top-left: small UPPERCASE Courier Prime eyebrow (or pill tag) in 40% ink.
3. One big IBM Plex Sans **Light** headline (2 lines max, sentence case,
   period at the end). Ink 100%.
4. One short supporting line at 45% ink.
5. One element of "life": a stat numeral, a tiny green dot + label, or a
   small data table mock.
6. Bottom: wordmark `CERTRACE` in Courier Prime, wide-tracked, 50% ink.
7. Optional: one dark `#111` rounded (12px) button-style CTA chip.

### Per format

- **Social posts:** the recipe above; one message per post; ≥ 15% padding on
  all sides; stat-led posts ("94%" huge, label small) perform the identity
  best.
- **Pitch decks / slides:** bone background every slide; one idea per slide;
  title slides = eyebrow + big light headline; data slides = mono tables/
  hairline charts; section dividers = pixel-glyph + tag badge. Dark `#111`
  slides allowed only as chapter breaks (max 1 in 10).
- **Event banners:** wordmark huge in Courier Prime (mimic the splash screen),
  headline beneath, node-network motif as faint background art.
- **Landing/product pages:** follow [UI_PATTERN_LIBRARY.md](./UI_PATTERN_LIBRARY.md)
  section-by-section.
- **Blog graphics:** 16:9 bone canvas, eyebrow = category tag, headline ≤ 8
  words, faint dotted-grid backdrop.
- **Print/promo:** uncoated paper stocks match the brand naturally; ink-only
  printing is on-brand; keep emerald as a spot accent.

**Fonts when unavailable** (e.g., Canva without uploads): IBM Plex Sans →
its free Google version (use Light 300); Courier Prime is free on Google
Fonts; Geist → Inter. Never substitute a serif or geometric display face.
