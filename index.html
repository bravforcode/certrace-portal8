01 What is Certrace

Certrace is a platform for exporters who need to report emissions data to meet CBAM requirements. Today that process runs on email threads and spreadsheets, chasing the same data from suppliers every reporting cycle. We're building the system that replaces that — starting with Thai steel, iron, and aluminum exporters.

02 The problem

The data lives in other people's inboxes. You're the one accountable for it, but you can't see what's complete, what's missing, or what's at risk until the deadline is close. Every cycle, the chase starts over.

03 What we're building

We're building the infrastructure to make CBAM reporting manageable, instead of a scramble every time a buyer asks for the report. We're early, and focused on getting this one thing right.
